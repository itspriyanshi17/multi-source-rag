# About NovaTech Electronics

NovaTech Electronics is an online retailer founded in 2019, specializing in consumer electronics
including laptops, smartphones, audio equipment, monitors, wearables, and accessories. The company
operates its own house brands (NovaTech, Pulsar, Pulse, Aurora, Zenbyte, Corevia) alongside a
curated selection of third-party products.

NovaTech's headquarters and primary fulfillment center are located in Austin, Texas, with regional
warehouses in Columbus, Ohio and Reno, Nevada to speed up delivery across the United States.

The company's mission is to make high-quality electronics accessible through transparent pricing,
straightforward policies, and responsive customer support. NovaTech serves both individual
consumers and small businesses through a dedicated business accounts program.

As of the most recent fiscal year, NovaTech employs approximately 340 people across support,
logistics, product design, and engineering.
