# Account & Membership FAQ

**Q: Do I need an account to place an order?**
A: No, guest checkout is available, but creating an account lets you track orders, manage
returns, and save payment methods.

**Q: What is NovaTech Plus?**
A: NovaTech Plus is a paid membership ($59/year) offering free Expedited shipping on all orders,
early access to new product launches, and an extended 45-day return window in place of the
standard policy.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the sign-in page. Reset links expire after 30 minutes.

**Q: Can I merge two accounts?**
A: Account merging isn't self-service; contact support with both account emails and we can merge
order history manually.

**Q: How do I close my account?**
A: Account closure requests can be submitted from Account Settings > Privacy, or by contacting
support. Order history is retained for legal/tax purposes even after closure.

**Q: Is there a business account option?**
A: Yes, business accounts unlock net-30 invoicing (subject to approval), bulk order tools, and a
dedicated account manager for orders exceeding $10,000/year.
