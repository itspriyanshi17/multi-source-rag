# NovaTech Rewards Loyalty Program FAQ

**Q: How do I earn points?**
A: Earn 2 points per $1 spent on all orders. NovaTech Plus members earn 3 points per $1.

**Q: What can I redeem points for?**
A: Points can be redeemed for store credit at a rate of 100 points = $1, or for exclusive
early-access drops on new product launches.

**Q: Do points expire?**
A: Points expire after 18 months of account inactivity (no purchases or point redemptions).

**Q: Can I combine Rewards points with NovaTech Plus benefits?**
A: Yes, NovaTech Plus membership and Rewards points are independent programs and stack together.

**Q: Do returns affect my earned points?**
A: Points earned from a purchase are reversed if that purchase is later returned or refunded.

**Q: Is there a referral bonus?**
A: Referring a friend who makes a purchase over $50 earns both accounts a one-time 500 point
bonus, credited after the referred order's return window has passed.
