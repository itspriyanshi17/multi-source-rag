# Payment & Billing FAQ

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: NovaTech accepts all major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and NovaTech store credit. Business
accounts may also apply for net-30 invoicing after an approval process.

**Q: When is my card charged?**
A: Cards are authorized at checkout but only charged once the order ships. For orders with
backordered items, each shipment is charged separately as it goes out.

**Q: I was charged twice for one order. What happened?**
A: This is usually a temporary authorization hold that will drop off within 3-5 business days and
not an actual duplicate charge. If both charges post permanently, contact billing support with
your order number.

**Q: Can I use multiple payment methods on one order?**
A: Split payments are supported only between store credit and one card or PayPal account.

**Q: Do you offer financing?**
A: Orders over $199 are eligible for installment financing through our third-party partner at
checkout, subject to a credit check.

**Q: How do refunds appear on my statement?**
A: Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically appear within 5-7 business
days, though your bank may take longer to post the credit.
