# Press Release: NovaTech Announces Aurora Smartwatch Pro

**AUSTIN, TX** — NovaTech Electronics today announced the Aurora Smartwatch Pro, the newest
addition to its wearables lineup, featuring an upgraded 10-day battery life, a brighter
always-on display, and expanded workout tracking with 60+ supported activity types.

"The original Aurora Smartwatch set a strong foundation, and Pro takes it further with the
battery life and display improvements customers asked for most," said NovaTech's VP of Product.

The Aurora Smartwatch Pro will be available starting next quarter at $199, with NovaTech Plus
members receiving 48-hour early access before the general on-sale date. Existing Aurora
Smartwatch owners will receive a 20% trade-in credit toward the Pro model through the NovaTech
trade-in program.

Pre-orders open two weeks before launch through the NovaTech website and all three flagship
showrooms in Austin, Columbus, and Reno.
