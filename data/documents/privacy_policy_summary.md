# Privacy Policy (Summary)

NovaTech Electronics collects account information (name, email, shipping address), order
history, and browsing behavior on novatech-electronics.example to operate the store, fulfill
orders, and personalize product recommendations.

## Data Sharing
NovaTech does not sell personal data to third parties. Data is shared only with service
providers necessary to fulfill orders, such as payment processors and shipping carriers, under
contractual confidentiality obligations.

## Data Retention
Order and account data is retained for 7 years after account closure to comply with tax and
financial recordkeeping regulations, even though most account features become inaccessible
immediately upon closure.

## Your Rights
Customers may request a copy of their stored personal data or request deletion (subject to the
tax recordkeeping exception above) by contacting privacy@novatech-electronics.example.

## Cookies
The website uses functional cookies (required for cart/session) and optional analytics cookies,
which can be disabled from the cookie preferences banner without affecting core site function.
