# Aurora Smartwatch — Product Manual (Excerpt)

The Aurora Smartwatch tracks heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), sleep, and over 40 workout types,
with up to 7 days of battery life on a single charge.

## Setup
1. Install the NovaTech Health app on your smartphone.
2. Power on the watch and follow the in-app pairing instructions.
3. Grant health and notification permissions to enable full functionality.

## Charging
Use only the included magnetic charging puck. Third-party chargers may not seat correctly and
can result in slow or failed charging.

## Water Resistance
The Aurora Smartwatch is rated IP67, meaning it can withstand splashes, rain, and brief
submersion, but is not intended for swimming or high-pressure water exposure such as showers.

## Warranty Note
The Aurora Smartwatch is covered under the standard 1-year limited warranty described in the
company warranty policy. Battery degradation is covered only if capacity falls below 70% of
original within the first year, consistent with NovaTech's general battery warranty terms.
