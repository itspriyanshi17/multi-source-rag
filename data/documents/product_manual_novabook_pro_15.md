# NovaBook Pro 15 — Product Manual (Excerpt)

The NovaBook Pro 15 is NovaTech's flagship laptop, featuring a NovaCore i9 processor, up to
32GB RAM, and up to 1TB SSD storage.

## Setup
1. Charge the device fully before first use (approx. 2 hours via the included 100W charger).
2. Power on and follow the on-screen setup wizard to create or sign in to your NovaTech account.
3. Registering your device during setup activates your warranty automatically.

## Battery Care
For best long-term battery health, avoid leaving the NovaBook Pro 15 plugged in at 100% charge
for extended periods. The adaptive charging feature (Settings > Power > Adaptive Charging) helps
manage this automatically.

## Return Note
Because the NovaBook Pro 15 ships with pre-installed licensed software that is activated on
first boot, **once the device has been powered on and registered, it is eligible for return
within 14 days only**, rather than the standard 30-day window described in the general return
policy. Unopened, unregistered units remain eligible for the full 30-day window.

## Troubleshooting
- If the device won't power on, hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a hard reset.
- Persistent overheating under normal use may indicate a fan obstruction; contact support.
