# Pulse Earbuds X2 — Product Manual (Excerpt)

The Pulse Earbuds X2 are true wireless earbuds with active noise cancellation and up to 8 hours
of battery life per charge (24 hours with the charging case).

## Pairing
1. Open the charging case near your device with Bluetooth enabled.
2. A pairing prompt should appear automatically; alternatively, hold the case button for 3
   seconds until the LED flashes white.
3. Select "Pulse Earbuds X2" from your device's Bluetooth menu.

## Controls
- Single tap: play/pause
- Double tap: skip forward
- Triple tap: skip backward
- Press and hold: toggle noise cancellation / transparency mode

## Care
Earbuds and case are IPX4 splash resistant but not fully waterproof; avoid submersion. Clean the
ear tips weekly with a dry cloth to maintain audio quality and hygiene.

## Return Note
As an audio accessory, the Pulse Earbuds X2 fall under the 15-day accessory return window
described in the general return policy, provided the ear tips are unused or in resalable
condition.
