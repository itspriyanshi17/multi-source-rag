# Shipping FAQ

**Q: What shipping options are available?**
A: NovaTech offers Standard (5-7 business days, free on orders over $50), Expedited (2-3 business
days, $12.99), and Overnight (1 business day, $24.99). Overnight orders must be placed before
2:00 PM Central Time to ship same-day.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: Currently NovaTech ships only within the United States and Canada. Canadian orders may be
subject to customs fees not included in checkout pricing.

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: A tracking link is emailed once your order ships, and is also available under Order History.
Tracking typically updates within 24 hours of the label being created.

**Q: My order shows "delivered" but I don't have it. What do I do?**
A: Wait 24 hours, as carriers occasionally mark packages delivered slightly early. If it still
hasn't arrived, contact support with your order number so we can open a carrier investigation.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?**
A: Address changes are only possible if the order has not yet entered the "Processing" stage.
Contact support immediately if you need to make a change.

**Q: Are backordered items shipped separately?**
A: Yes. If your order contains both in-stock and backordered items, in-stock items ship
immediately and backordered items ship as a separate package at no extra shipping cost.
