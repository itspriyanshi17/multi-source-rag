# Store Locations & Hours

NovaTech Electronics is primarily an online retailer, but operates three flagship showrooms
where customers can view products in person before ordering online for delivery (showrooms do
not hold retail inventory for walk-out purchase).

## Austin, TX (Flagship)
123 Congress Ave, Austin, TX 78701
Mon-Sat: 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Sun: 12:00 PM - 6:00 PM

## Columbus, OH
88 High Street, Columbus, OH 43215
Mon-Sat: 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Sun: Closed

## Reno, NV
450 Virginia St, Reno, NV 89501
Mon-Sat: 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Sun: 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Showroom staff can help with product demos, trade-in evaluations, and in-person warranty claim
drop-offs, but cannot process on-site returns for online orders — those must still be initiated
through the Order History page.
