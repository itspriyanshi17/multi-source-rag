# Support Ticket #10501 — Laptop Return Request

**Customer:** I bought a NovaBook Pro 15 three weeks ago and already registered it. I want to
return it now because it's too heavy for me. Is that still possible?

**Agent:** I checked your order and it looks like the device was registered 20 days ago. Per the
NovaBook Pro 15 product manual, once a unit is powered on and registered, the return window is
14 days rather than the standard 30-day policy, due to the pre-installed licensed software being
activated on first boot. Unfortunately that means this unit is just outside the return window.

**Customer:** That seems really short compared to your general 30-day policy, I didn't know that.

**Agent:** I understand the frustration — this exception is specific to the NovaBook Pro line and
isn't very visible at checkout. As a one-time courtesy, I can offer 80% store credit instead of a
standard return, given you're only 6 days past the deadline.

**Customer:** I'll take the store credit, thank you.

**Resolution:** 80% store credit issued as a courtesy exception outside the standard laptop
return window. Ticket closed.
