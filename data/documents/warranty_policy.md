# Warranty Policy

All NovaTech house-brand products (NovaTech, Pulsar, Pulse, Aurora, Zenbyte, Corevia) include a
**1-year limited manufacturer warranty** covering defects in materials and workmanship under
normal use. This warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or unauthorized
repairs.

## Extended Warranty
Customers may purchase NovaCare+ extended protection at checkout or within 30 days of purchase,
extending coverage to 2 or 3 years and adding one incident of accidental damage protection per
year.

## Filing a Warranty Claim
1. Contact support with your order number and a description of the defect.
2. Support may request photos or a video of the issue before approving a claim.
3. Approved claims are resolved via repair, replacement, or store credit, at NovaTech's
   discretion, typically within 10 business days of receiving the returned unit.

## Laptop-Specific Note
Laptop batteries are covered for reduced capacity only if capacity falls below 70% of original
within the first year; battery wear beyond that is considered normal and is not covered.

## Monitors
Monitors with more than 3 stuck or dead pixels within the first 90 days qualify for replacement
under the standard warranty; fewer than 3 is considered within normal manufacturing tolerance.
